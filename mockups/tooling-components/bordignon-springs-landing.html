Why Customers Choose Bordignon

Proven advantages that translate to real savings and better tooling performance.

⚡ Highest Power Density

The Black Series delivers exceptional force in the smallest possible envelope. More clamping force per mm of housing diameter than any Raymond equivalent — lets you design smaller, lighter tooling.

🔧 Four Load Classes in One Footprint

Oval Series gives you USB → USV (Medium → Extra Strong) all in the same housing diameters. Select the right load without redesigning your die set.

🏭 100% Made in Italy

Every spring is manufactured start-to-finish at Bordignon's 5,000 m² factory in Rossano Veneto, Italy. Full material traceability, no subcontracted production.

🔍 Laser-Marked Traceability

Every spring is laser-marked with its specifications — never wears off like ink stamping. Full quality chain visibility from factory floor to your press.

📦 Local Stock — No Wait

Now stocked at Tooling Components in Erie, PA and New Jersey. No transatlantic shipping delays — same-day or next-day availability for standard Oval Series and Black Series SKUs.
