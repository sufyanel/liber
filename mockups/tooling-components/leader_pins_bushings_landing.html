What Are Leader Pins & Bushings?

Leader pins (also called guide pins) and their mating bushings are the primary alignment system in every injection mold, die cast tool, and stamping die. Mounted on the A-side and B-side mold halves, they guide the core into the cavity with precise registration — typically within 0.001" – 0.003" clearance — ensuring consistent part wall thickness, preventing cavity damage, and extending tool life. Without reliable leader pins and bushings, even the most carefully machined mold will produce scrap.

🎯

Critical Alignment

Leader pins provide the first point of contact as the mold closes, guiding both halves into precise alignment before any cavity contact occurs.

🛡️

Wear Protection

Case-hardened steel pins (58–60 HRC) paired with bronze or steel bushings absorb thousands of open/close cycles with minimal wear.

📏

Multiple Configurations

Straight, shoulder, and guided ejection styles in inch and metric DIN standards — plus multiple bushing materials and mounting styles.

Materials & Specifications

Pin Materials & Finishes

4150 Alloy Steel (Standard)

Industry standard for leader pins. Case-hardened to 58–60 HRC with a tough core. Centerless ground OD for precise fit. Available in standard and guided ejection lengths.

H13 Tool Steel

Upgraded material for die cast and high-heat applications. Superior hot hardness and thermal fatigue resistance. Nitrided surface available for extended service life.

Surface Finish

All leader pins are centerless ground and polished to a 8–16 micro-inch finish for smooth engagement and minimal bushing wear.

Bushing Options

Hardened Steel Bushings

Maximum wear life for high-production tools. ID case-hardened to 58–60 HRC. Precision-honed ID with lead-in chamfer for smooth pin entry.

Solid Bronze Bushings

SAE 660 bearing bronze. Self-lubricating structure prevents galling with steel pins. Excellent for clean-room and medical mold applications where lubricants are prohibited.

Ball Bearing Bushings

Recirculating ball design provides zero-clearance alignment. Preloaded to eliminate side play. Ideal for high-cavitation molds, thin-wall packaging, and precision medical tooling.
