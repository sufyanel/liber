What Are Ejector Pins?

Ejector pins are precision-ground rods that push finished plastic or die-cast parts out of a mold cavity after the material has cooled and solidified. They are among the most critical — and most economical — components in any injection mold or die cast tool. A pin failure can stop production, damage a cavity, or ruin hours of mold-making labor. That is why choosing the right material, coating, and tolerance is not optional — it is essential to keeping your line running.

⚙️

Critical to Mold Performance

Ejector pins directly affect cycle time, part quality, and overall tool reliability. A quality pin means fewer rejects and less downtime.

📐

Precision-Ground Tolerances

Centerless ground diameters held to ±0.0002" ensure proper fit, prevent flashing, and extend pin life through thousands of cycles.

🔩

Materials & Specifications

Material Options

H13 Hot-Work Tool Steel

Industry-standard for injection molding and die casting. Excellent thermal fatigue resistance, toughness at elevated temperatures, and wear resistance. Core hardness 47–52 HRC with nitrided surface 65–70 HRC.

M2 High-Speed Steel

Preferred for ejector blades and high-wear applications. Through-hardened to 58–62 HRC for superior edge retention in thin cross-sections.

Stainless Steel

Corrosion-resistant option for medical, food-grade, and clean-room molding environments. Available in inch and JIS metric.

Beryllium-Free Copper Alloy

10× better thermal conductivity than steel for performance core pins. Reduces cycle time in heat-sensitive applications. 90–98 HRB.

Coatings & Surface Treatments

Nitrided

Surface hardness 65–74 HRC. Reduces wear, minimizes galling, and extends pin life. Available on straight, shoulder, and step pins.

Through-Hardened

Uniform hardness from core to surface. Ideal for small-diameter pins where surface-only treatment is insufficient.

Armor Coated

Advanced PVD/TiN coating for extreme wear resistance. Effective in abrasive-filled resins and high-cavitation tools.
